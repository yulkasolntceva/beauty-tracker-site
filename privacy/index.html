Privacy Policy

Beauty Tracker & Planner

Last updated: September 2026

Your data stays on your device

Beauty Tracker & Planner is designed with privacy in mind. Your procedure records, photos, notes, prices, appointment information, and other content you add to the app are stored locally on your device and are not sent to our servers.

Data you add to Beauty Tracker

Beauty Tracker does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit the content you add to the app, including:

  • Procedure names, notes, or comments
  • Prices
  • Photos attached to records
  • Contact details you enter
  • Appointment dates and schedules

This information is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes the data stored within the app. Any backup files you previously exported remain wherever you chose to save them.

Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect app usage and technical information. This helps us understand how Beauty Tracker is used and improve the app.

Firebase Analytics may collect:

  • App usage events, such as when certain features are used
  • App session information
  • Basic technical information about the app and device

Beauty Tracker also logs limited events such as when a record or category is created, a backup is created, or certain reminder features are enabled.

We do not include the content you enter into Beauty Tracker in analytics events. Procedure names, notes, prices, photos, appointment details, and other record content are not sent to Firebase Analytics.

Firebase Analytics data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Face ID / Touch ID

If you enable biometric lock, Face ID or Touch ID is used to authenticate you when opening the app. Authentication is handled by iOS. Beauty Tracker does not access or store your biometric data.

Photos

You can choose photos to attach to your procedure records. Selected photos are stored locally within the app's private storage and are not uploaded to our servers or included in analytics.

Notifications

Beauty Tracker uses local notifications for appointment and other reminders you choose to enable. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No record content is sent to our servers to deliver them.

Backup

Beauty Tracker includes a manual backup feature that exports your data as a file. The backup is created locally, and you choose where to save or share it using the options provided by your device. We do not receive or have access to your backup files.

Children's privacy

Beauty Tracker is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Beauty Tracker's features or data practices change. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
